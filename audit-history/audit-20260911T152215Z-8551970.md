**Date:** 2026-09-11 15:22 UTC

**Status:** PASS — documentation drift corrected

## Summary

Product design refinement of the sessions sidebar. Three user-facing changes:

1. **Recency buckets** — top-level sessions sorted newest-first with date dividers ("Today", "Yesterday", "Last 7 days", "Last 30 days", "Older"); teammate rows remain nested and unshelved. New `recencyBuckets.ts` module with `RecencyBucket` type and `recencyBucket()` helper.

2. **Project eyebrow on cards** — when the sidebar shows all projects, each card prints a favicon + project name above the title; when scoped to one project, the eyebrow is hidden and the preview names the project instead. Props `showProject` and `projectPath` added to `SessionCard`.

3. **Attention pill in focused scope picker** — when the sidebar is focused on a project, the masthead displays a warning pill counting sessions in other projects that need attention. New `WaitingCount` component and `waitingElsewhere()` helper in `ProjectScopePicker`.

4. **Untitled session fallback** — sessions with no title, prompt, or slug now display "Untitled session" instead of their raw sessionId. New constant `UNTITLED_SESSION_TITLE` and `isUntitledSession()` helper in `sessionListView.ts`.

5. **Separator removed** — the `Separator` divider under the `AttentionStrip` was deleted (it's now shelved by recency dividers instead).
